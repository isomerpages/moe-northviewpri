<p><strong>Discover North View Primary School through our E-Open House!</strong>
</p>
<p>We warmly invite you and your family to discover how we bring our mission
and vision to life each day. Guided by our commitment to <strong>nurture learner agency and deepen competencies through meaningful, coherent learning in a caring and enabling environment</strong>,
we strive to <strong>Inspire Growth, Empower Learning, Shape Tomorrow</strong> for
every child.</p>
<p>Begin by watching our <strong>Principal's Message</strong> to learn more
about the beliefs that shape learning at North View Primary School. Then,
take a virtual tour of our school to explore our learning spaces and discover
the environment where our students learn, grow, and thrive.</p>
<p>We hope this E-Open House offers you a glimpse into the North View experience
and how we partner with families to support every child's learning journey.
We look forward to welcoming your family to our school community.</p>
